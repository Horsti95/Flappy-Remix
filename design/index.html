Ordinal RGB decay next

Body lightness across signup_index 0..50000
Continuous decay from white to near-black across the player base. Tier boxes mark the cosmetic bands (first-50 / 51-500 / 501-5,000 / 5,001-50,000), but the actual RGB shifts smoothly within each band so two players at indices 1,000 and 4,999 still look distinct.

Daily twist modifiers next

Per-day modifier candidates
One modifier per UTC day, picked deterministically from the daily seed so it's the same for everyone worldwide. Physics-only ones are 1-2h to build each; the visual ones (fog / night / sunset) are a half-day each. Headwind is the only mechanical one in this batch.
